## Feature list (for reference)

**Wall:** drag, turn (corner grip or two fingers), pinch to resize, lock in
place, stacks, alignment guides, tidy up / arrange by colour, threads with
labels and arrows, draw on the wall with a marker, zoom and pan past the
screen, double-tap to focus a note, drag a note onto a tab to move it, lasso
multi-select with bulk pin / recolour / move / delete, Undo pill.

**Notes:** light formatting (bold, italic, bullets), checklists with progress,
link notes, drawings on plain / ruled / grid / dotted paper, photo prints with
captions and frames, tape labels, voice notes as cassettes, emoji, pin to top,
reminders (one-tap presets, any date and time, daily / weekly / monthly).

**Boards:** unlimited boards, each with its own wall or photo, emoji icon,
reorder by drag, All boards overview with pictures of each board, Archive,
45 templates, 43 theme packs (preview before applying, Undo), memory walls
from up to 20 photos.

**Views:** wall, grid (2 columns on phones, up to 6 on tablets), list with
swipe to delete; search across all boards, accent-insensitive ("tuoi" finds
"Tưới"); filter by kind; sort in grid and list.

**Look:** 31 walls, procedural stains and marks (can switch off), "Calm the
wall" slider, Lights off night mode (warm, never grey; on / off / system /
schedule), 16 fonts, try Pro walls / fonts / pins on the real wall before
buying.

**Share:** export a board as an image or PDF (whole wall or view, all or
selected notes, 2–4×, crop), share or save a single note as an image, share
text / links / photos from other apps into a board.

**Home-screen widget (Android):** a board's pinned or newest notes with paper
dots and to-do progress, tap a line to open the note, + for a new note, or a
picture of the board; colours and text size set in the app.

**Safety:** Trash (30 days), weekly automatic backup, backup to file and
restore with a preview (add or replace, with Undo), Android backup carries
notes to a new phone, app lock (fingerprint / face / screen lock), locked
boards (Pro), optional sync through the user's own Google Drive (Android).

**First run:** a short guide with a practice wall, then a blank wall, a
template or the sample wall; *How to use* manual in Settings → Help.

## Words to avoid

From the store listing rules, and true for the site too:

- "encrypted", "vault", "100% on-device", "never leaves your phone" (sync and
  the phone's own backup can copy data off the device)
- a hard-coded price (the store sets it per country)
- "subscription" for Pro (it is one payment)
- claims about iPhone features that are Android-only (sync, widget actions)
