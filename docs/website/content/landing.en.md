## Section: Move them like paper

**Heading:** Move them like paper

Drag a note anywhere. Turn it with a corner grip, or with two fingers. Pinch
to resize. Stack a few into a pile, lock one in place, tidy the whole wall
into neat rows in one tap. Every move can be undone.

- Wall runs well past the screen edges: pan and zoom like a real board
- Alignment guides click a card into line with its neighbours
- Tidy up, or arrange by paper colour
- Undo for moves, turns, resizes and tidies (40 steps)

## FAQ

**Is Sticky Wall free?**
Yes. Every feature above the Pro section is free, with no ads. Pro is an
optional one-time purchase.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Open the app and start writing.

**Where are my notes stored?**
On your phone, in the app's private storage. Sticky Wall has no servers.

**Can I use it on more than one phone?**
On Android, switch on *Sync across devices*: your boards are kept the same on
every Android device signed in to the same Google account, through a hidden
folder in your own Google Drive. You can also move everything with a backup
file.

**What happens if I change phones?**
Android's own backup carries your notes to the new phone. You can also make a
backup file in *Settings → Backup & restore* and load it on the new phone.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Everything works without a connection; sync catches up later.

**Is it in Vietnamese?**
Yes, fully: the app, the fonts (every one carries the full Vietnamese
alphabet) and search, which finds "Tưới" when you type "tuoi".

**Is Pro a subscription?**
No. You pay once and keep it. Restore it on a new phone with the same store
account.

**Is there an iPhone version?**
*[Answer depends on the owner — see README. Suggested: "Sticky Wall is on
Android today. An iPhone version is planned."]*

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete notes and boards in the app, use *Settings → Delete all data*, or
uninstall the app. If you used sync, delete the copy in Google Drive's
settings (*Manage apps → Sticky Wall → Delete hidden app data*).
