## 3. Looking after your data

Your notes live on your device, so their safety is in your hands:

- Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your notes from that

- The app can back up everything to a file, and it keeps an automatic weekly

  with it.
- Deleted notes wait in the Trash for 30 days, then are removed for good.

We cannot recover notes for you: we never had a copy.

## 4. Sync across devices (optional, Android)

If you switch on *Sync across devices*, the app copies your data to a hidden
app folder in your own Google Drive and reads it back on your other Android
devices signed in to the same Google account. That copy is stored by Google
under Google's own terms and privacy policy. A deletion made on one device
also syncs to the others, so sync is not a replacement for backups. You can
switch sync off at any time, and delete the copy in Google Drive's settings
(*Manage apps → Sticky Wall → Delete hidden app data*).
